Privacy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

Agentic Learning Studio turns a topic into a personalized, interactive lesson. It is currently a free beta — we are not charging. This notice explains, in plain terms, what we collect, why, and how to reach us. It is operated by Anand Pareek, an individual resident in India.

What we collect

  • Your email, used to sign you in and keep your lessons with your account. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider; we never see your password.
  • Preferences you set (such as level and goals) to personalize lessons.
  • What you submit to build a lesson — your prompt and any document or repository link you attach.
  • Lessons you generate, plus ratings and which sections you’ve opened, so you can return to them and so we can improve the lessons.

Attached documents and repositories are held only in memory for your working session to ground that lesson — they are not stored as files. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not collect payment card details.

Why we collect it

To run the service, to build and save your lessons, and to improve the product. We use a small number of service providers to operate the app (for example, to host it, store your lessons, generate lesson content, and send the occasional account email). We share only what is needed for them to perform that function.

Accessing or deleting your data

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} (or use Report issue / complaint) and we’ll take care of it.

Security & children

We describe the practices in place on our Security page. The service is intended for adults (18+). This is an early beta, so this notice may change as the product evolves.
