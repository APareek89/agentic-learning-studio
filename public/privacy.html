Privacy Notice

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Wizbit / PrathibhaX creates personalized, AI-generated interactive lessons. This notice explains what data the Service processes, why, how long, and the choices and rights you have. The Service is operated by Anand Pareek, an individual resident in India, under the brand name Wizbit / PrathibhaX.

1. What we process

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

  • Account & auth — your email, and password/authentication metadata managed by our auth provider (we do not see your raw password).
  • Profile preferences — role, level, goals, and similar settings you provide to personalize lessons.
  • Learning inputs — your prompts, uploaded documents, and repository (repo) URLs.
  • Generated content — the lessons, blueprints, diagrams, and quizzes produced for you.
  • Feedback & progress — ratings, comments, and which modules you’ve opened.
  • Contributor & community data — your display name, bio, and lessons you publish to Community courses.
  • Support & complaint data — what you send via the report/complaint form.
  • Payment metadata — only if and when paid plans are enabled later (handled by a payment provider; we do not store card numbers).

2. What we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone data, Aadhaar, PAN, payment card numbers, CVV, or other unnecessary sensitive personal data. Please don’t put such data into prompts, uploads, or the complaint form.

3. Uploaded documents & repositories

Documents and repo content you attach are used to ground your lessons in your own material. Today, uploaded content is held in process memory (see src/lib/uploads.ts) for the working session and is not written to long-term storage as raw files. The generated lesson artifacts you build are persisted in our database (the lessons table) so you can return to them.

4. Retention

  • Generated lessons and blueprints are currently retained for up to 1 year unless you delete them earlier.
  • Community-shared lessons are public snapshots and remain visible until you remove them or they are hidden.
  • Support/complaint and consent records are kept as needed to handle your request and to meet legal obligations.

5. Why we process it (purpose)

To provide and personalize the Service, generate and store your lessons, ground lessons in your material, run community features, respond to complaints and data requests, secure the Service, and meet legal obligations.

6. Processors / sub-processors

We use trusted service providers to run the Service. As applicable, these include:

  • Anthropic — AI model provider that generates lesson content.
  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Render — application hosting.
  • Langfuse — request tracing/observability, if enabled.
  • An email provider (e.g. Resend / Postmark) — to deliver support and notification emails, if enabled.
  • A payment provider — only if paid plans are added later.

7. Consent

Where we rely on your consent, we ask for it clearly (for example, the “I agree to the Terms and acknowledge the Privacy Notice” checkbox at sign-up, which is never pre-ticked) and we record it.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may exercise the following — submit any of these via Report issue / complaint or findkailash@gmail.com:

  • Withdraw consent at any time (this may limit features that depend on it).
  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion / erasure of your data, subject to legal retention needs.
  • Grievance redressal with our Grievance Officer.
  • Nomination — you may nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

Grievance Officer / Data Protection Contact: Anand Pareek — findkailash@gmail.com.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 18 unless verifiable parental consent and appropriate child-safeguards are implemented. We do not knowingly process children’s data for behavioural tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising. If you believe a child has used the Service without consent, contact us and we will act.

10. Security

We apply technical and organisational safeguards described on our Security page (HTTPS, security headers, locked-down CORS, authentication, row-level security, rate limits, sandboxed lesson rendering, and more).

11. Data breaches

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify affected users without undue delay and notify the Data Protection Board as legally required. Where applicable, we will provide detailed breach information within 72 hours.

12. Changes

We may update this notice; material changes update the “Last updated” date above. These business and legal details may be updated if the app is later operated through a registered entity.
